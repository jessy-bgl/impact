**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Impact app (hereby referred to as "Application") for
mobile devices, published as an Open Source project. The Application is provided "AS IS".

**Who is responsible for your data?**

The data controller is **Jessy Tony Bogalho**, acting as a natural person, not as a company.
You can contact the controller for any privacy question at **impactech@proton.me**.

**What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?**

The Application works without a user account. Your answers to the carbon-footprint
questionnaire (diet, transport, housing, etc.) are processed **only on your device** and are
never transmitted anywhere — only the names of the categories and sub-categories you have
completed are sent as analytics, never the values of your answers. You can delete them from
your device at any time, from within the Application; see "What are my rights?" below.

With your consent, given through an in-app toggle before any data leaves your device, the
Application sends two kinds of data to **PostHog**, an analytics and error-tracking service:

- **Product analytics** — which screens and features are used, so the Application can be
  improved. This includes: a random identifier generated on your device, the screens you
  navigate to, which app features you interact with (e.g. which questionnaire category you
  open, whether you complete it), your device model, operating system version, app version,
  language and timezone, and your approximate country/region inferred from your IP address.
- **Crash and error diagnostics** — technical error messages, so bugs can be identified and
  fixed. These messages are deliberately written to never contain your questionnaire
  answers.

No data is collected before you give your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time from
the same in-app toggle. If you decline or withdraw consent, no analytics or error data is
sent, and any identifier already stored on your device is deleted.

**On-device identifier**

If you consent, a random identifier is stored on your device to let PostHog recognise
repeat visits (e.g. distinguish a new session from a returning one). This identifier is
**not renewed periodically**: it is kept until you uninstall the Application, clear its
data, or withdraw your consent — whichever comes first. It does not identify you by name,
email, or any other directly identifying information.

**Does the Application collect precise real-time location information of the device?**

No. The Application does not use any device location API and does not know your precise
location. If you consent to analytics, PostHog infers your **approximate** country/region
from your IP address (standard internet routing information) — this is coarse geolocation,
not precise device location.

**Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?**

Only if you consent to analytics. In that case, the data described above is sent to
**PostHog, Inc.** (2261 Market St #4008, San Francisco, CA, USA), acting as a data
processor. It is stored in PostHog's EU Cloud region, in Frankfurt, Germany. Because
PostHog, Inc. is a US-based company, this involves a transfer of data outside the European
Union; it is covered by Standard Contractual Clauses under a signed Data Processing
Agreement (dated 2026-07-27). PostHog's own sub-processors (cloud hosting and
infrastructure providers) are listed at https://posthog.com/subprocessors.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising purposes.

**How long is data kept?**

- Analytics events: **1 year**.
- Crash and error reports: **90 days**.
- The on-device identifier: as described above, until uninstall, data clearing, or
  consent withdrawal.

**What are my rights?**

Under the GDPR and French data protection law, you have the right to:

- **Give or withdraw consent** at any time, from the in-app toggle — this is the primary
  and easiest way to control your data.
- **Erase the data held on your device**, yourself and without contacting anyone, with the
  "Effacer mes données locales" button on the **Paramètres → Mes données** screen. This
  permanently deletes your questionnaire answers, your computed emissions and your saved
  actions from this device. It does **not** delete analytics events already sent to
  PostHog — for those, use the contact route below. Your analytics consent choice is
  deliberately kept, so that clearing your data never silently re-enables or disables
  collection behind your back; change it with the toggle on the same screen.
- **Access, rectify, restrict, object to, or export** the analytics data associated with
  your device, and to request its **erasure**.
- To exercise these rights, contact **impactech@proton.me**. Because there is no user
  account, please include your device's identifier (shown on the **Paramètres → Mes
  données** screen) so the request can be matched to the right data. If you have already
  withdrawn consent or cleared the app's storage, that identifier — and the ability to
  match a request to it — is lost; this is a real limitation and not a promise we can get
  around. We aim to respond within one month.
- **Lodge a complaint with the CNIL** (the French data protection authority), at
  https://www.cnil.fr/fr/plaintes, if you believe your rights are not respected.

**What are my opt-out rights?**

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time from the in-app toggle, without uninstalling
the Application — this stops all further collection immediately. Uninstalling the
Application also stops collection and removes the on-device identifier, but is no longer
the only way to opt out.

**Automated decision-making**

The Application does not use your data to make any automated decision that produces legal
effects or similarly significantly affects you, and does not build a profile of you beyond
the anonymised, aggregate analytics described above.

**Special categories of data**

The Application does not knowingly collect data revealing racial or ethnic origin, health,
religious beliefs, sexual orientation, or other special categories of personal data. Your
questionnaire answers — which could touch on some of these (e.g. diet) — never leave your
device.

**Children**

The Application is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data
from them. In France, the digital age of consent for the processing of personal data is
**15 years old**; if you are under that age, please ask a parent or legal guardian to give
consent to analytics on your behalf. If you believe a child has given consent without
appropriate parental involvement, please contact us at impactech@proton.me.

**Security**

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect the data described above, including
transmission over encrypted connections and reliance on PostHog's own security
certifications. No system is perfectly secure, and no absolute guarantee can be made.

**Changes**

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by
updating this page and the "effective as of" date below; continued use after a material
change constitutes acceptance of the update, but does **not** by itself constitute new
consent to any additional data collection, which is always requested separately through the
in-app toggle.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-07-27.
